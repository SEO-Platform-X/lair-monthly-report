What we need from you

Two things speed everything up.

1. Reviews

Ask every happy patient for a review.

Velocity is king.

AI weighs how often people review you, not just the star rating. A steady stream every week beats a burst once a year.

Show this on your phone.

Patients scan it and land on your review page. Save the image to your phone and keep it on your home screen.

How med spas get the most reviews
  1. Ask at the mirror. Right after treatment, while they're looking at the result, is the moment. Not by text the next day.
  2. Make it one sentence. "Would you mind leaving us a quick review? Here, just scan this." Then hold up the QR.
  3. Name the treatment. A review that says "microneedling with Maria" teaches AI what you're known for. A review that says "great place" doesn't.
  4. Have every provider do it. One ask per patient, every day. That's the whole system.
2. Photos

Send us photos. Any photos.

This moves the needle more than most people expect. AI reads what's in a photo, and we add the code behind each one: the location, the treatment, the words in the image, the name of the provider. That tells AI and Google exactly what you do and where.

Phone photos are perfectYour space, your team, before and afters you have consent for, signs, menus, certificates. Anything with words in it is gold.
